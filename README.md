## Understanding the Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence

Before diving into an AI course, it's crucial to familiarize yourself with the core concepts. AI encompasses various fields, including machine learning (ML), natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, and robotics. Here's a breakdown of some fundamental terms:

- **Machine Learning**: A subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data and improve over time without explicit programming.
- **Deep Learning**: A specialized area of ML involving neural networks that mimic the human brain, suitable for processing large datasets.
- **Natural Language Processing**: Focuses on the interaction between computers and human language, allowing machines to understand, interpret, and respond to human speech or text.

   ```python
   import nltk
   from nltk.chat.util import Chat, reflections

       ['hi', ['hello', 'hi there']],

   ]

   chatbot = Chat(pairs, reflections)
   chatbot.converse()
   ```
